## Interpretation policy

BioSignal keeps genotype and phenotype as separate evidence types. A detected AMR gene or mutation is not treated as proof of phenotypic resistance. The analysis must verify that genomic and AST evidence refer to the same isolate before making an isolate-level concordance claim.
